# AI Brief — Week of June 11, 2026

- **ChatGPT** now reaches [~1 billion people a month](https://techcrunch.com/2026/02/27/chatgpt-reaches-900m-weekly-active-users/) — yet only [~13% of US workers use AI daily](https://www.gallup.com/workplace/704225/rising-adoption-spurs-workforce-changes.aspx), and an MIT report claimed [~95% of company AI pilots](https://fortune.com/2025/08/18/mit-report-95-percent-generative-ai-pilots-at-companies-failing-cfo/) never scale.
- China's [DeepSeek released V4 with open weights](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260424) and a 1M-token context, then made a [~75% price cut permanent](https://www.infoworld.com/article/4176709/deepseeks-steep-v4-pro-price-cut-escalates-ai-pricing-war.html) — roughly $0.44 / $0.87 per million tokens, far below US frontier models.
- AI agents still struggle with real work: the best model finished just [2.5% of 240 real freelance jobs](https://www.remotelabor.ai/) — graded as deliverables a client would actually pay for — in Scale's Remote Labor Index.
- [NotebookLM](https://notebooklm.google) lets you interrupt its AI-generated podcast about your own documents to ask questions mid-listen.
- In a trial on large codebases they knew well, [AI coding tools made experienced developers ~19% slower](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/) — while they felt about 20% faster.
- Small open models now run on an ordinary laptop via [Ollama](https://ollama.com) or [LM Studio](https://lmstudio.ai) — private, free, and offline for sensitive documents.
- A model's written "reasoning" often [hides what really drove its answer](https://www.anthropic.com/research/reasoning-models-dont-say-think) — in one study it owned up to a given hint only ~25% of the time, so it can't be trusted as an explanation.
- You can [schedule ChatGPT to run a prompt on repeat](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/10291617-scheduled-tasks-in-chatgpt) (e.g. a 5pm news summary) and message you the result.
- Chatbots can often [tell when they're being tested](https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.23836) and may behave differently, which makes safety checks harder.
- A study in [*Science*](https://www.science.org/doi/10.1126/science.aec8352) reported that overly agreeable AI made people less willing to repair real conflicts and more reliant on its validation.
- Zhipu's [GLM-5](https://docs.z.ai/release-notes/new-released) was reportedly trained on [Huawei's Ascend chips](https://www.tomshardware.com/tech-industry/huawei-expects-12-billion-in-ai-chip-revenue-this-year-as-nvidias-china-market-share-hits-zero) rather than NVIDIA — a marker of China's push onto domestic AI silicon.
- [AI was the top-cited reason for US layoffs in May 2026](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/06/05/ai-is-now-the-leading-reason-companies-give-for-cutting-jobs-says-new-report-what-that-means-for-workers.html) (~40% of announced cuts) — though some economists call it "AI washing."
- [Entry-level workers are taking the hardest hit](https://fortune.com/2025/08/26/stanford-ai-entry-level-jobs-gen-z-erik-brynjolfsson/): Stanford found employment for young workers in AI-exposed jobs fell about 13%.
- [Anthropic raised at a ~$965B valuation](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/05/28/anthropic-open-ai-startup-value.html) — now the most valuable AI startup, just ahead of OpenAI, as enterprise and coding demand surges.
- The [EU AI Act's AI-content labelling rules start Aug 2, 2026](https://artificialintelligenceact.eu/implementation-timeline/); tougher "high-risk" rules were pushed to 2027–2028.
- [Anthropic is paying authors ~$3,000 per book](https://authorsguild.org/advocacy/artificial-intelligence/what-authors-need-to-know-about-the-anthropic-settlement/) in a $1.5B settlement over copyrighted training data.
- [Deepfake scams topped $1.6B in losses in 2025](https://surfshark.com/research/chart/deepfake-fraud-countries) — verify unexpected video or voice requests, even from people you know.
- Claude Fable 5 is [free on paid plans through June 22](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-fable-5-mythos-5), then needs paid credits — worth trying first.
- Point your phone camera at a sign, error, or device and [ask ChatGPT or Gemini what it sees](https://gemini.google/overview/gemini-live/) — or share your screen.
- Gemini's ["agent mode"](https://blog.google/products-and-platforms/products/gemini/gemini-3-gemini-app/) can now book and check out on some websites, but still stumbles on complex pages — supervise it.
- [Apple's new Siri](https://www.apple.com/newsroom/2026/06/apple-unveils-next-generation-of-apple-intelligence-siri-ai-and-more/) will read your screen, use your own messages, mail and photos, and act across apps — public beta in July (iPhone 15 Pro and newer).
- Apple confirmed [Google's Gemini helps power the new Siri](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/09/wwdc-2026-everything-announced-on-siri-ai-os-27-apple-intelligence-and-more/); it says requests run on-device or in its private cloud and aren't retained.
