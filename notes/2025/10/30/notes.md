### Overview

In today’s breakfast, we covered topics such as advanced AI coding tools like Kilo code and MCP servers, vector databases versus traditional search for education platforms, AI-assisted hiring challenges and the growing complexity of social media distribution, and hands-on testing of tools like Figma AI, Canva, and video editing automation. An interesting finding was that vector databases, despite their popularity in 2023, often prove more complicated than traditional search solutions like Elasticsearch for most practical applications, with one educator switching away from his initial vector store setup after discovering simpler alternatives worked better.

### AI-Powered Development Tools and Workflows

- Advanced coding assistance through specialized tools
  - Kilo code plugin integrates with VS Code and allows switching between different AI models
  - Manages agent tasks automatically without manual prompt engineering
  - Particularly effective for amateur developers bridging the skill gap
- Model Context Protocol (MCP) servers enable tool integration
  - Allow AI agents to interact with databases and external systems
  - More reliable than vector embeddings for structured data queries
  - Require traditional database setup rather than specialized vector stores

### Database Architecture: Vector Stores vs Traditional Search

- Vector databases face practical limitations in real applications
  - Difficult performance assessment and debugging compared to traditional solutions
  - Character limits and chunking requirements create maintenance overhead
  - Tool calls with Elasticsearch often prove faster and more reliable
- Traditional search engines offer clearer advantages
  - Elasticsearch indexing cheaper than vector embeddings
  - Keyword-based search with stemming handles most use cases effectively
  - 30,000 records easily manageable without specialized vector database infrastructure
- Semantic search still valuable for specific applications
  - Finding conceptually similar content without exact keyword matches
  - Example: Locating photosynthesis passages that don’t contain the word “photosynthesis”
  - LLMs can generate appropriate search terms for traditional databases

### Education Technology and AI Question Generation

- Test preparation platforms struggle with AI-generated content quality
  - Current AI models fail at creating compelling wrong answer choices for multiple choice questions
  - Generated questions lack the subtle trickiness that makes practice valuable
  - Template-based approach shows more promise than direct generation
- Question templating methodology offers better results
  - Extract patterns from existing high-quality questions
  - Create intermediate format describing question structure and logic
  - Generate variations using proven templates rather than free-form creation
- Student engagement tools require careful AI integration
  - RAG systems can provide relevant similar questions during tutoring sessions
  - Teacher interfaces need efficient question discovery and modification capabilities
  - AI grading shows potential but requires human oversight for quality assurance

### Hiring and Human-AI Collaboration Challenges

- AI provides limited value in recruitment processes
  - Lacks intuitive judgment for candidate assessment
  - Cannot replace human partnership in business development
  - Requires extensive verification of all outputs, creating additional work
- Human expertise remains irreplaceable in teaching contexts
  - Teachers model high-level engagement with subject matter
  - Students need human examples of achieved expertise for inspiration
  - AI instruction lacks the inspirational quality of human mastery demonstration

### Social Media Distribution and Business Strategy

- Distribution has become the primary challenge for new products
  - Technical barriers to entry significantly lowered by AI tools
  - Social media spam and bot farms make organic reach extremely difficult
  - Established influencers maintain advantages while new creators struggle to break through
- B2B distribution offers more viable pathways than B2C
  - LinkedIn maintains less spam-filled environment than consumer social platforms
  - Professional networks still allow relationship-based business development
  - Domain expertise combined with technical capability creates sustainable competitive advantages

### Creative AI Tools and Practical Applications

- Video editing AI shows mixed results across different use cases
  - Automated editing adequate for simple product demonstrations
  - Human editing still superior for emotional storytelling and artistic expression
  - Tools like [ChatCut](https://www.chatcut.io) enable chat-based video editing workflows
- Design automation delivers immediate practical value
  - Figma AI creates job posters and marketing materials from prompts
  - Canva AI generates event invitations and simple graphics quickly
  - $20 price point common across many professional AI design tools
- Content creation requires balancing efficiency with quality
  - AI-generated content identifiable by overly smooth, emotionless writing style
  - Human content maintains natural ups and downs with emotional resonance
  - Budget constraints often drive AI adoption despite quality trade-offs

### Tool Ecosystem and Business Models

- Subscription bundling becoming standard practice
  - [Lenny’s Product Pass](https://lennysproductpass.com) offers access to multiple AI tools for $200-350 annually
  - Individual tool subscriptions often exceed bundle cost
  - Distribution partnerships benefit both tool creators and bundle providers
- Enterprise AI frameworks gaining adoption
  - [BMAD method](https://github.com/bmad-code-org/BMAD-METHOD) provides structured prompts for business development
  - System creates adversarial AI agents that challenge ideas rather than simply agreeing
  - Long-form prompting enables deeper business analysis and planning

### Future of Education and Skill Development

- Foundation skills become more critical as AI capabilities expand
  - Reading comprehension and logical thinking irreplaceable by automation
  - Students must experience developing expertise in some domain to understand human achievement
  - University education should focus on problem-solving frameworks rather than specific technical skills
- Alternative education models show promising approaches
  - Alpha School combines 2-hour AI curriculum with experiential learning
  - Outdoor and hands-on learning environments like Green School in Bali create positive associations with education
  - Traditional Chinese education system may not prepare students for uncertain technological future
