# AI Breakfast #15 - November 6th Meeting Recap

## 💰 **AI Subscriptions & Workarounds**

- Attendees discussed creative ways to access premium AI tools affordably:
  - One member uses a US-based Android phone and Google account to bypass regional restrictions (see [Change Google Play country](https://support.google.com/googleplay/answer/7431675)).
  - Group buys and educational discounts were mentioned (e.g., $20/month for [Gemini Advanced via Google One AI Premium](https://one.google.com/about/articles/google-ai-for-students/)); see the [GitHub Student Developer Pack](https://education.github.com/pack).
  - Student verification challenges noted – many tools require US-based student status.
  - [Community college enrollment](https://www.cccapply.org/) ($1,000/unit in CA) floated as a potential workaround

## 🛠️ **AI Productivity Tools**

- **Coding & Development:**
  - Mixed reviews on AI coding assistants – speed vs. code quality concerns
  - [Cursor](https://www.cursor.com/) and [Windsurf](https://codeium.com/windsurf) praised for understanding codebases
  - Prompt engineering frameworks (like "[Constitutional AI](https://www.anthropic.com/research/collective-constitutional-ai-aligning-a-language-model-with-public-input)" method) discussed for better outputs
- **Research & Analysis:**
  - [Perplexity](https://www.perplexity.ai/) favored for factual accuracy and source citations
  - [ChatGPT-5](https://chat.openai.com) improved but still produces incorrect source links sometimes
  - One member uses AI for financial modeling (volatility forecasting) with Python
- **Everyday Uses:**
  - Voice-controlled cooking assistant for real-time recipe help
  - Resume optimization debate – should employers require AI-generated applications?
  - Note-taking tools ([Granola](https://granola.so/)) for interview transcription

## 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 **AI & Parenting**

- Screen time concerns dominated:
  - Short-form content ([TikTok](https://www.tiktok.com/)) seen as attention-span killers
  - Preference for long-form content (films, structured learning)
  - Car AI systems (e.g., [Li Auto](https://www.lixiang.com/)'s voice assistant) unexpectedly engaging for kids
- Education approaches:
  - [Alpha School](https://www.alpha.school/) model discussed: 2-hour AI-driven academics + project-based afternoons
  - [Khan Academy](https://www.khanacademy.org/) recommended as free alternative
  - Debate over early AI exposure (5-year-olds)

  - Multi-user "bump" system (like digital fist bumps)
